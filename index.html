About Santa Madara Neilande

Santa Madara Neilande is a graphic designer and illustrator from Latvia. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Arts and Humanities from Liepāja University and a Digital Media Practitioner certificate from Baltic Computer Academy. Her work is graphic design: vector graphics, logos, posters, brochures, brand kits and Twitch / VTuber graphics such as banners, overlays and emotes, as well as digital character illustration.

She works in Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects and Premiere, Clip Studio Paint, Inkscape and Blender. Alongside design she is learning DevOps and has made a digital painting app (Seviathstudio) and World of Warcraft addons (SeviathsUsefultools).
