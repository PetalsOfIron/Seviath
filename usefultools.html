SeviathsUsefultools

World of Warcraft addons made by Santa Madara Neilande

About

SeviathsUsefultools is a collection of World of Warcraft addons that adds handy tools to the game: a farming pack, a codex, dungeon & raid helpers and guild tools. They are free, open source and available for two versions of the game. Pick the one that matches your client.

To install, download the addon, unzip it and put all the folders of the zip (SeviathsUsefulTools and the SeviathsUsefulTools_... data folders) into your Interface/AddOns folder of the matching game version.

Retail (Midnight)
